Lifehood
Los Angeles · 2020
Before Lifehood, I created The NOW massage.
It grew to more than 100 locations.
Then I lost the business I built.
It was ruthless.
I refused to let it change what I believed about people.
I wanted to build around what I still believed.
A system that made it easy to give good, feel good and spread the love from one person to the next.
Did you know?
Giving benefits the giver, not only the receiver.
And being treated with kindness can make people more likely to pass that kindness on.
I believed business could create profit and positive impact at the same time.
So I built Lifehood to find out.
143 people participated. ~70% chose someone else. No incentive.
I wasn’t building a growth channel.
I was looking for proof.
What I found was both.
The business was discovered inside the human behavior.
Love It Forward was born.
